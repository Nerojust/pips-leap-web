Pause and settings
How do I finish a path?

Reach the Moon Gate at the end. Each path gives up to 3 stars: one for finishing, one for finding every firefly and one for losing no lives. Stars unlock coats, scarves and accessories in the Wardrobe.

Where can I learn what everything does?

The first paths teach everything step by step, and How to play explains every control, creature and pickup at any time.

The buttons are too small or too big

Change them in Settings → Touch controls → Button size.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game works offline. Only cloud saves and ads need a connection.

Progress & cloud saves

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in to Game Center on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play Games on Android. The game keeps a copy of your progress in that account and restores it on any device signed in to the same account. You can check the status in Settings → Saved progress.

Without it, progress stays on the device.

My progress didn't come back after reinstalling

Make sure you're signed in to the same Game Center or Google Play Games account as before, then open Settings → Saved progress and wait a moment while it syncs. If it still doesn't appear, email support.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Settings → Saved progress → Reset progress, then tap again to confirm. This can't be undone.

Ads & privacy

When do ads appear?

Only when you choose to watch one to keep going after game over. You can always start the path over instead. The desktop version has no ads.

No ad is offered when I run out of lives

You may be offline, or no ad is available right now. Try again later, or start the path over.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the game: Settings → About → Privacy choices (ads). On iPhone and iPad, tracking permission is in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

What data does the game collect?

Pip's Leap has no accounts and the developer collects no personal information. Google collects some data to show ads. Read the full privacy policy.

Problems & bugs

I found a bug

Email support with what happened, which path you were on and, if you can, a screenshot or screen recording. It helps a lot.

There's no sound

Check the volumes in Settings → Sound, and that your device's volume is turned up. If it still doesn't play, email support.
