# Pip's Leap

A cosy, hand-crafted platformer about Pip, a little fox spirit, gliding through the twilight on
a lantern-lit scarf. 30 paths across 6 worlds, a story told by Wick the lantern moth, and a
Wardrobe of coats and scarves to earn with stars.

**▶ Play in your browser: https://nerojust.github.io/pips-leap-web/**

Works offline once loaded, and can be installed as an app from the browser menu. Keyboard,
touch and game controllers all work.
