Pip's Leap is a single-player game with no accounts. The developer does not collect any personal information. The game does show optional ads, provided by Google, and Google collects some data to deliver them. This page explains what, and how you can control it.

What the game stores, and where

The game saves the following on your device only:

  • your progress: unlocked levels, best scores, fireflies found and stars earned;
  • your settings: volumes, tips, vibration, touch button size and scarf colour.

The developer never receives this information. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings → Saved progress → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app (on the web: by clearing this site's data in your browser).

Cloud saves (optional)

On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, and on iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, the game also keeps a copy of your progress in that account so it can be restored on another device or after reinstalling. This copy is stored and handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies; the developer of Pip's Leap cannot see it. You can turn this off by signing out of Google Play Games or Game Center.

Ads

When you run out of lives, you can choose to watch an ad to keep going from the last lantern you lit. Ads are only shown when you ask for one; you can always start the level over instead. Ads come from Google AdMob in the Android and iPhone/iPad apps and from Google AdSense on the web. The desktop app has no ads.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad service may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, if allowed);
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • how you interact with ads, and basic device and app information;
  • crash and performance information about the ad service;
  • on the web, cookies or similar technologies.

Google handles this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The developer of Pip's Leap does not receive this data, only totals of how many ads were shown.

Keeper Pack (optional purchase)

In the Android and iPhone/iPad apps you can buy the Keeper Pack once. The payment is handled by Google Play or the App Store; the developer never sees your payment details. To confirm the purchase and restore it on a new device, the game uses RevenueCat, which keeps a record of the purchase against an anonymous ID it creates (no name, email or account). See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Sharing

The Share button on the results screen opens your device's own share sheet with a short message about the path you finished. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Your choices about ads

  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland (and where other laws require it), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → About → Privacy choices (ads) (in the apps) or with the privacy link Google shows on the web version.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, the advertising ID is not used and ads are less personalised. The game works exactly the same either way. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • You can also control personalised ads at My Ad Center.

Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

Delete your data

Pip's Leap has no accounts, and the developer holds no data about you. To delete what exists:

  • Progress and settings on your device: Settings → Saved progress → Reset progress, or uninstall the app (on the web: clear this site's data).
  • Cloud saves: delete the game's saved data in your Google Play Games or Game Center account (Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, or iOS Settings → Game Center).
  • Ad data: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings; Google explains its deletion options in the Google ads policy.
  • Keeper Pack purchase record: email the address under Contact and it will be deleted from RevenueCat within 30 days. (A deleted record can't restore the pack on a new phone.)

Any other request: email quandorxit@gmail.com.

Internet access

The game can be played offline. It goes online only for the optional cloud saves above and for ads; with no connection, ads simply aren't offered. The web version is loaded from its website like any web page.

Children

The developer collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The game is not directed at children under 13. Parents who do not want a child to see ads can play the desktop version, or simply not choose the ad when it is offered.

Third parties

The only third parties are Google (ads, and Google Play Games cloud saves), Apple (Game Center cloud saves) and RevenueCat (Keeper Pack purchase records), as described above. There are no analytics or social features.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
